# Beck's Sober Almanac

My 2026 journey to sobriety from cannabis — counted in collective sober days, not a single "quit date."

**Read my journal:** https://beckymaehayes.github.io/sober-journal/

**Start your own (free template):** [`starter-template.html`](starter-template.html)

---

## Why this exists

The all-or-nothing framing of a "quit date" never worked for me. One slip would wipe out the counter, and wiping out the counter always felt like permission to give up. So I count *every* free day of the year instead — a hard day doesn't erase the clean ones behind it, and it isn't a reason to stop.

This repo has two things in it:

1. **My journal** (`index.html`) — the read-only version of the tool I use most nights, with my real, unedited entries.
2. **A blank starter template** (`starter-template.html`) — the same app, stripped of all my data, for *you* to use for your own recovery from anything.

## Make it your own — it's free, that's the point

You don't need to know how to code, and you don't need to ask an AI to build it. The working code is right here. Take it:

1. **Download** [`starter-template.html`](starter-template.html) (open it, then use the download/raw button).
2. **Try it first** — just double-click the file to open it in your browser. It runs entirely on your own computer. Name what you're stepping back from, pick a start date, and check in.
3. **Want it online, like mine?**
   - Make a free account at [github.com](https://github.com).
   - Create a new **public** repository.
   - Upload the file and rename it `index.html`.
   - In the repo's **Settings -> Pages**, set the source to *Deploy from a branch* (main / root).
   - In a minute or two, GitHub gives you your own free public link.
4. Or **fork this repo** (the "Fork" button up top) to copy the whole thing into your own account in one click, then swap in the starter.

Your entries are saved only in your own browser, private to you, until *you* decide to share them.

## What the template does

- Counts your collective free days across the year, through every season.
- A daily check-in: free or used, how the day felt, what pushed back, what helped, and space to write.
- A seasonal ring, running streaks, and gentle milestones.
- **Export / import** so you can back up your entries or move them between devices.
- No account, no server, no tracking. One file. Everything stays on your device.

## Tech notes

- One self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no framework, no server.
- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Data lives in the browser's `localStorage`.
- Hosted free on GitHub Pages.

## A note on the content

My journal entries describe real substance use, cravings, and hard mental-health days, in my own words. They're personal and unpolished on purpose. If you're working through something similar, be gentle with yourself while you read — and know that starting messy is still starting.
